# Story Bible — SPOILERS

> Don't read this if you want to play the mystery blind.

## The truth

- **Killer:** Kotone Amane, owner of Café Lantern.
- **Motive:** Ten years ago her younger sister, **Hinako Amane**, a Seiran Academy Class 2-B student, died in an "accidental fall" from the school roof after reporting abuse. The four victims each helped bury it:
  - Michiko Tanabe (pharmacist) supplied sedatives found in Hinako's system and said nothing.
  - Goro Ueno (then school board, now councilman) pressured police to close the case.
  - Emi Kurosaki (school nurse then) falsified the infirmary record.
  - Haruto Ishida (Hinako's homeroom teacher) ignored her reports, and later regretted it — which is why he tutors poor students like Yuki for free.
- **The cranes:** pages from the Class 2-B **class diary** Hinako kept, dyed red. Kotone is folding her way toward 1,000 — her "wish" is that everyone involved pays.
- **The bell:** a tiny silver bell charm on Kotone's apron string (Hinako's). It rings when she moves quickly.
- **Ink on Ishida's palm:** "7-3", room 7-3 = the old Seiran rooftop stairwell storeroom where the diary was hidden; he scribbled it, intending to tell someone.
- **Pocket crane:** Kotone slipped it into Ren's coat while hanging it up at the café.
- **Why Ishida trusted the killer:** he recognised Hinako's sister and agreed to meet her to apologise ("someone from the old days").
