# Story Bible — SPOILERS

> Don't read this if you want to play the mystery blind.

## The truth

- **Killer:** Kotone Amane (29), owner of Café Lantern.
- **The dead girl:** ten years ago, Kotone's younger sister **Hinako Mori** (17, Seiran Academy class 2-B) fell from the roof of the school's old annex on **14 June**, in the rain. It was filed as an accident.
  - Their parents divorced when Hinako was nine. Hinako took their mother's surname, Mori; Kotone kept their father's, Amane. This is why nobody connects the two names.
- **What really happened:** Principal **Sadao Kirishima** ran "guidance" sessions, locking students he considered troublemakers alone in **room 7-3**, a windowless storeroom at the top of stairwell 7 in the old annex. Students called it the reflection room.
  - Hinako documented it in a private notebook, with dates and other students' accounts.
  - When she threatened to go to the newspapers, Kirishima met her on the annex roof, grabbed her by the wrists, and she fell.
  - He has always told himself he "let go".
- **The cover-up.** Each of the four victims helped bury it:
  - **Michiko Tanabe** (pharmacist) sold unprescribed sedatives to the school nurse, used to keep "reflection room" students quiet. They were in Hinako's blood. She said nothing.
  - **Emi Kurosaki** (school nurse) gave Hinako the pills, then removed Hinako's infirmary visits from the record.
  - **Goro Ueno** (school board, later councilman) pressured the police to close the case within three days.
  - **Haruto Ishida** (Hinako's homeroom teacher) was told everything by Hinako and answered "don't make trouble". He signed a statement saying she showed "no signs of distress". He regretted it for ten years, which is why he tutored poor students like Yuki for free.
  - Kirishima's falsified alibi (a conference in Kyoto) kept him out of the file entirely.
- **Inspector Hayama** was the young detective who closed the case. Hinako's sister came to the station every day for a month; he told her to go home. On the last day she left a white paper crane on his desk. He never learned her first name.
- **Kotone** collected Hinako's belongings from the school, including the class 2-B **class diary** Hinako had taken home that week. She moved to Mizunagi, opened Café Lantern near the station, and over seven years let Inspector Hayama, a regular, talk about old cases. She learned every name in the file.
- **The cranes** are pages of that class diary, dyed red. Hinako taught Kotone to fold cranes: a thousand cranes grant one wish. Kotone has been folding toward a thousand. Her wish: that someone would finally listen to her sister.
- **The bell** is a tiny silver bell charm. Kotone gave it to Hinako "so I can always hear you coming". Hinako wore it on her school bag; after her death Kotone ties it to her apron string, and it rings when she moves quickly.
- **Ishida's murder:** Kotone asked to meet him as "someone from the old days". He recognised her and came to apologise. Before leaving home he wrote **"7-3"** on his palm, meaning to tell her where Hinako hid her proof. She cut his throat from behind before he could tell her.
- **Pocket crane:** in Chapter 1, Kotone hangs Ren's wet coat by the heater and slips the crane into it.
- **The fifth target:** Kotone only learns Kirishima's part because Ren finds the notebook in Chapter 3 and, exhausted, it reaches her at the café. Ren's own slip puts Kirishima on the roof.

### Chapter 2 — Class 2-B
1. **Police office, morning.** Ren hands in the pocket crane. Hayama suspects Aya, who came through the door that night. Mikami: the page is from a *class diary* (gakkyū nisshi); it's dated 9 June ten years ago, and the student on duty that day signed "Mori".
2. **Hub:** Ishida's flat, Seiran Academy.
   - **Ishida's flat:** a ten-year-old class 2-B photo with one girl circled ("Hinako. Forgive me." on the back), and an unsent letter: *"She told me where he put them. I said: don't make trouble."*
   - **Seiran:** Yuki (Saturday club). The ghost story of the girl who fell from the old annex. Annex rooms are numbered by stairwell, so **7-3** is the "reflection room". The annex is locked.
3. **Aya storms the office.** Interrogation (secrets):
   - She was a first-year in the newspaper club; Hinako asked the club to publish, and Aya was too scared.
   - Two months ago a red crane came in her mail: *"Ask them about Hinako Mori."*
4. **Archive:** Hinako Mori's case file. It names Tanabe, Kurosaki, Ueno, Ishida and Principal Kirishima (away in Kyoto), and the investigating officer, **D. Hayama**. The receipt for her effects is signed for by *"elder sister, K."*
5. **End:** a red crane on Hayama's desk.

### Chapter 3 — The Reflection Room
1. **Interview room.** Hayama interrogation:
   - Ueno pressured him to close the case; he did, and got promoted.
   - The sister came every day for a month (a university student, short hair, glasses, "Mori-san"). She left a white crane.
2. **Morgue.** Mikami interrogation:
   - As a trainee she saw grip bruises on both of Hinako's wrists and sedatives in her blood; her boss wrote "accident".
   - The Crane's blade is a short, thin, single-edged kitchen knife (a petty knife), used by someone who cuts things every day.
3. **The old annex at night,** with Yuki. Stairwell 7, room 7-3. Under a loose floorboard: **Hinako's notebook** (CG).
   - Entries: the reflection room; the nurse's "calming pills"; Ishida's "don't make trouble"; *"I taught Onee-chan to fold cranes. She's hopeless. A thousand for one wish."*; *"Onee-chan gave me her bell so she'd always hear me coming."*
   - Last entry (14 June): *"Principal Kirishima wants to meet me on the roof after school. I'll tell him I made copies. Tomorrow I give this to Onee-chan."*
4. **Café, 2 a.m.** Ren is exhausted. Choice: tell Kotone about the notebook, or keep quiet. If he keeps quiet, Hayama arrives and says it anyway. Kotone: *"Then someone should make him say it out loud."*
5. **Kirishima's house, morning.** He refuses protection. A red crane is pinned to his door.

### Chapter 4 — One Thousand Cranes
1. **Interview room.** Kirishima interrogation:
   - He was on the roof; he grabbed her wrists; "she pulled away".
   - The sister came to his house a month later, with Hinako's bell jingling on her bag. He had her removed.
2. **Café, afternoon.** Yuki returns the annex key. Kotone hurries over with cake, the bell rings, and Yuki goes white: *"That sound."* Kotone laughs it off and leaves on an "errand".
3. **Aya calls:**
   - A family register shows Kotone Amane's younger sister is **Hinako (Mori)**.
   - Her stakeout photo of Café Lantern was taken Friday at 22:31: lights off, CLOSED sign. Kotone came back at 23:40, soaked, with a dark umbrella.
4. **21:40.** Kirishima has slipped his guard. Race to the Seiran roof (choice of route: stairwell 7 is fastest).
5. **Roof (CG):** Kirishima is slashed but alive, clutching crane **#999**. A figure in a dark raincoat drops onto the fire escape.
   - Choice: chase (Kirishima nearly bleeds out; Ren turns back) or stay.
   - On the roof: the **silver bell**, its string cut.
6. **1 a.m., café.** Kotone, hair wet, wipes the counter. No bell on her apron. *"You lost something tonight, Kotone-san."*

### Chapter 5 — The Thousandth Crane
The accusation. Kotone rebuts; Ren presents evidence or answers a deduction. A wrong answer gets a counter and a retry.

| Kotone says | Ren answers with |
|---|---|
| "I'm just a café owner." | Family register |
| "I was here all night." | Aya's photo (café closed 22:31) |
| "I went for a walk. Bells are everywhere." | Yuki's bell under the bridge, then the bell from the roof |
| "That charm could be anyone's." | Deduction: Hinako's bell (notebook and Kirishima) |
| "Aya was at the door when the crane got into your coat." | Deduction: Kotone hung the coat by the heater |
| "Where would I even get those pages?" | Effects receipt, "elder sister, K." |

Then comes the confession (CG). Hayama: *"…The white crane. You were Mori-san."*

The choices and endings:
- **Choice 1:** let her fold the thousandth crane, or take her in now. This changes the closing lines.
- **Choice 2:** give a copy of Hinako's notebook to Aya, or leave it to the prosecutors.
- **Ending A — "The Thousandth Wish"** (Aya publishes): Kirishima is charged, Hayama testifies, and Yuki and Ren visit Hinako's grave. If Ren let her fold the crane, her wish is revealed: *"that someone would finally listen to her."*
- **Ending B — "Closed File"** (prosecutors only): Kirishima's lawyers bury it, and Ren gets a commendation. A white crane appears on Ren's desk.

## Red herrings
- **Dr. Mikami** is right-handed and calm with blades. She worked on Hinako's autopsy, which she hid.
- **Aya** knows too much, too early. She was at the café door the night of the pocket crane, and Hinako was her schoolmate.
- **Hayama** closed the original case, and becomes a target himself.

## Fair-play clues (the player can solve it before Ren)
- Ch1: Kotone's sister taught her cranes; the silver charm on her apron string; she hangs Ren's coat.
- Ch2: the effects receipt is signed "elder sister, K."
- Ch3: the notebook mentions the cranes and the bell; Mikami's kitchen-knife profile.
- Ch4: the bell at the café; Kotone's "errand".
